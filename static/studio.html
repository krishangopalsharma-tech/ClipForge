ClipForge Studio

Transcribe locally → let Claude or Gemini pick the moments → render here.  ·  Back to the main app  ·  Storage

1 Load the video & subtitles

Upload your episode. A subtitle or timed-text file (.srt, .vtt or .txt, including Premiere Pro exports) supplies the exact words, and its timing is then checked against the audio.

1. Choose video or drag here
mp4, mov, mkv, webm, avi …
2. Optional: Add subtitles or timed text
.srt, .vtt or .txt (Premiere timecode works)

2 Transcribe

Runs local Whisper on your GPU. Skipped instantly if this video was done before.

3 Send to Claude, ChatGPT or Gemini

Download the transcript, copy the prompt, paste both into a new chat. Long transcripts: attach the .txt file instead of pasting it.

The prompt asks for segment ids, never timestamps. Models drift on numbers; ids get resolved against the transcript here, so clips land exactly on the words.

4 Paste the reply

Paste whatever it sent back — prose and code fences are fine.

Want the full caption, music and effects controls? Send these clips to the main app and render them there with the live phone preview — everything you normally use still applies.

The video loads itself over there — nothing to upload again, and no re-transcribing. Just style it and press Generate.

5 Render

Cuts, reframes and burns captions with ffmpeg. Local, same as always.

⬇ Download all clips + CSV (ZIP) CSV only

6 Edit your clips

Change framing, captions, music, look, logos or the cut of any finished clip. Only the clip is rendered again: nothing is re-downloaded or re-transcribed, and no AI is called.

Needs the source video and its transcript to still be on disk (Storage page). Clips made before this feature existed cannot be edited; generate them again.
